ACTOR SCOPE
===========

Primary actor:
  Operations Coordinator

Modeled as system data, not mandatory interactive actors:
  - Food donors
  - Recipient organizations
  - Delivery vehicles

Optional future actors:
  - Donor representative (if donor portal is added)
  - Recipient representative (if recipient portal is added)
  - Driver (if a driver portal is added)
